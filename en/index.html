Turning Japan’s value
into ventures

Social Finance Japan (SFJ) is a general incorporated association that finds value hidden in Japan’s regions, culture and technology, helps turn it into real ventures, and brings it to the world.

WHAT WE DO

How SFJ helps

SFJ does not run the ventures itself. We bring together the three things a new venture needs to get started.

01

Putting the funding together

CAPITAL

We bring in funding from foundations, investors and others, and structure it to fit each venture.

02

Designing how it works

BUSINESS DESIGN

Who runs it, what it does, and how it can last — we design the venture’s framework together.

03

Planning the use of digital

DIGITAL DESIGN

How data and AI can serve the venture, considered from the planning stage.

SFJ is a medium that connects Japan’s value to ventures. Each venture is run by its own team; SFJ takes part as a catalyst that helps it get off the ground.

02

PROJECTS

What we are working on

Initiatives and ventures SFJ is helping to launch.

Regional & cultural initiatives

SOUL PRIDE

Recording local cultures and the people who carry them on, and sharing them with the world.

SOUL JAPAN

Creating opportunities for people and companies from abroad to visit Japan, experience it, and build something together.

JAPAN VILLAGE

Creating places where people from around the world can experience Japanese life, culture, food and hospitality.

SMART LOCATION

Using forests, energy and AI to create new industries and hubs in local communities.

AI & technology ventures

ENGAGE NETWORK

A service in which AI finds and delivers meaningful connections between people. Launching in 2027.

TAZUNA

Preparing a system that lets people use AI agents with peace of mind.

OUR GOAL

Toward a society
where trust circulates.

Each venture takes root in its community, and its results feed the next. Over 30 years, we aim for a “Trust Society” in which people, regions, capital, technology and culture are connected by trust.
